## Long (Facebook About, LinkedIn, directories with a description field)
SalaahDaily is a free prayer times website for 2,800 cities in 196 countries, in 18 languages including English, Urdu, Arabic, Hindi, Bengali, Indonesian, Malay and Turkish.

Every time is calculated from the sun's position at the city's coordinates, using the method followed in that country, such as Karachi for Pakistan and India, Umm al-Qura for Saudi Arabia, JAKIM for Malaysia and ISNA for North America. The Hanafi, Shafi'i, Maliki, Hanbali and Jafria schools are all supported.

Each city page shows today's five prayers with a live countdown, a monthly timetable, the Qibla direction and the Hijri date. The site also has Ramadan Sehri and Iftar timetables, Eid dates, the 99 Names of Allah, the 99 Names of the Prophet ﷺ, and the Quran and hadith on each prayer's time and benefits.

No accounts, no sign-up. Run by a small independent team. Corrections are welcome at team@salaahdaily.com.
